SUPPORT

STEVEN STOREN is an interactive 3D rotary telephone experience for iPhone. Pick up the handset, turn the dial and explore sounds, animations and haptic feedback.

For help, bug reports or privacy questions, contact Steven Wobbes at hallo@stevenwobb.es. Please include your app version, iPhone model and a description of the issue. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

Using the app

The telephone experience runs on your device. The optional listening-archive connection is disabled by default and requires access to the configured server. It does not provide ordinary telephone calling or emergency calls.

PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: 7 October 2026

This policy describes the STEVEN STOREN iPhone app, provided by Steven Wobbes.

Local use

The app’s 3D visuals, dial interactions, sound effects and haptic feedback run on your device. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking or a user-account system. It does not record microphone audio or access contacts or location.

Motion input

The app uses device motion to detect shaking and trigger visual effects. Motion readings are processed on the device and are not sent to the developer or archive server.

Optional archive connection

When you explicitly enable the archive connection in Settings, the app contacts the HTTPS server configured there to check availability and request that phone 1 rings. It does not send the digits dialed in the 3D phone. The server necessarily receives connection information, such as the network address, and the request. This setting is disabled by default; turning it off prevents new archive requests.

The developer does not intentionally retain personal information from these commands. Server and network-provider logging may depend on the configured server and its operator; this policy does not promise that all infrastructure is log-free.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to send. We use this correspondence to answer your request and troubleshoot issues. You can contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Your choices and contact

You can use the local experience without enabling the archive connection. For privacy questions or requests, email hallo@stevenwobb.es.

This website

Changes
